AI consciousness and moral status

Thomas Nagel, newly awarded the Berggruen Prize, gave Wired an interview in which he argues against AI consciousness. Commentators quote his view that data centers share nothing with the physical character of a nervous system and that language alone is not a natural manifestation of consciousness. Frank Pasquale and Mor Naaman argue in Tech Policy Press that AI is an artifact, made rather than born, and that granting it rights would shift power to those who make and control it. This is a political argument about who benefits, separate from the metaphysical question. A dissenting note: one commenter says the question is legitimately open because we understand consciousness so poorly and already debate it for animals, infants and hypothetical aliens.
