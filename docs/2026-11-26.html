Delegating thought and the value of human work

One question is what humans retain when AI does the intellectual work, whether through delegated math problems or research design. A research finding reported this week says frontier models now propose research designs that graders rate above a leading expert's, while their research questions rate as less valuable, so human judgment about what to ask may be what remains. Economic accounting is also contested, with one essay arguing GDP misses gains from customized products and another asking whether people should learn what AI does poorly.
